Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TREND ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit buildwithtrend.com or buy our products. Questions? Email hello@buildwithtrend.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name and email when you buy a product, join our list, or contact us.

How we use it

  • to deliver the products you buy and provide customer support;
  • to send you receipts, product updates, and (if you opt in) marketing emails;

  • to prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and tracking pixels, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics, to understand traffic and measure our advertising. These tools may set their own cookies and receive data about your visit. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, such as:

  • MailerLite (email, checkout, and product delivery);
  • Stripe (payment processing);
  • Meta and Google (analytics and advertising).

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email. You may request a copy or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@buildwithtrend.com.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our products, meet legal and accounting requirements, and resolve disputes.

Children

Our products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes
